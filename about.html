The workshop behind the name

Renewed isn't a slogan. It's how we work.

We started Renew Riders because buying a used vehicle shouldn't feel like a bet. Here's what that means in practice.

Our story

Renew Riders is a small, independent business focused on buying and selling used bikes and cars.

We carefully check the vehicles we deal with and provide clear information about their condition and paperwork. When a vehicle needs some extra work, we also offer restoration services to bring it back to a better condition.

We're starting small, with every vehicle handled personally and every customer treated honestly. Whether you're looking to buy a used bike or car, or want to sell your vehicle, we're here to make the process simple and straightforward.

Renew Riders — Used Vehicles. Restored Right.

  • Inspected, not just cleanedEvery vehicle goes through a documented mechanical and paperwork check before it's listed.
  • Fair, upfront pricingWhat we quote is what you pay — no last-minute "inspection fees" or hidden charges.
  • We handle the paperworkWant to avoid the registration transfer and documentation hassle? We'll take care of the paperwork for you.
Who's turning the wrench

A small team that stands behind every sale.

"We inspect every vehicle the way we'd want one inspected for our own family. If we wouldn't ride it home, it doesn't go on the floor."

— Renew Riders, Founding Team

Renew Riders is run by a small crew of mechanics, inspectors and a couple of people who genuinely enjoy the paperwork nobody else wants to do. We keep the team small on purpose — it means the person who inspects your bike is the same one you'll speak to on WhatsApp.

We're always happy to walk you through a vehicle's history in person before you decide. Come by, or send us a message first — either way works.
